from the invite to the mic

No sign-up sheet at the bar. Scan, pick your song, watch the screen.

    Get the invite

    Scan to sing

  3. 3

    Watch the screen

house rules

Only two.

  • No judgment

    No scores, no rankings, no side-eye. Off-key counts. Forgetting the second verse counts. Everybody in this room is on your side.

  • Sing your favourites

    We're here to have a good time and perform the songs we love. Pick the one you know every word to and let the room sing it with you.
